# R1 state-to-current physical validation V1

Status: development protocol; no independent R1-2 exit approval. This is the
current authority for evidence revision six. The V1/V2 operator dispositions
and EvidenceV5 describe historical stages and cannot authorize current results.

## Physical experiment

Keep the approved R1 reference, material parameters, physical control volumes,
A-D controls and all existing physical accuracy limits. Preparation is a shared
dark zero-bias D equilibrium. A-D retain the same populations at switching;
freeze selected rates without removing their charge or stored population.
Allowed interval counts are 16,32,64,128,256. Independent time triples are
(1,2,4),(2,4,8),(4,8,16),(8,16,32),(16,32,64), and nonlinear factors are
1,0.1,0.01,0.001. A recorded execution setting is not a convergence certificate.
The existing short functional window and the explicitly declared logarithmic
windows use the exact times generated by the versioned observation-time code.

## Recomputed content

Save exact accepted solver coordinates and the references needed to decode
them, actual time, duration, voltage and refinement level. Recreate the same
initial algebraic event and reevaluate each saved coordinate through the
physical equations, including incremental storage. Recompute populations,
Poisson and local equations, drift/diffusion and ion currents, trap capture,
charge continuity, physical boundary displacement, regular electrode current,
and the applicable certificate metrics. Compare the saved reports to these
fresh values. Recalculation is a diagnostic pass, not another time integration.
Initial and accepted states retain the original Jacobian and operator checks.
Historical optimizer trials cannot be inferred from accepted states and must
not be described as independently replayed.

An honest failed trajectory remains failed. Validate the saved prefix and its
reported failure separately from whether every physical accuracy limit passed.
Absent coordinates or missing partial state are explicit unavailable checks,
never fabricated zeros. A successful aggregate or repeated run cannot replace
per-state and per-component verification.

## Time and scope

Match the declared time triple, window, output count, true times, control
switches, material stack, fixed reference and numerical policy to executed
arrays and the fixed study inputs. No record can promote a single trajectory
to a converged matrix, a complete frequency range or an independently approved
mechanism result. Revision six may execute the declared extended settings in
controlled mode, but scientific acceptability still depends on the actual
physical checks and later independent refinements.

## Long-window uncertainty

The existing relative eliminated-operator limit remains 1e-6. It is not
replaced by a floor, a larger threshold or an automatic weak-signal pass.
Prior private instrumentation produced conflicting late normalized diagnostics
(0.6667 versus 1.5); neither is an established universal/asymptotic value.
Production evidence must retain each component's direct and eliminated
values, absolute discrepancy, normalization scale and original relative value
at the same saved coordinate before interpreting that conflict.
A small net flux is not proof of accurate or inaccurate physics by itself.
Dimensioned error budgets and a replacement criterion require separate
analytic/high-precision validation and review.

## Scientific limits and legacy records

Only the current physical acceptance route can claim that the declared
per-case physical checks passed. Revisions one through five are inspection-only;
explicit legacy inspection reports historical status and never grants current
physical acceptance. Startup failures may be inspected for intact records but
cannot be accepted as controlled calculations when their required inputs are
absent. Inspection errors identify missing fields instead of pretending that
all source or physical checks ran.

Environment, timestamps and earlier optimizer history remain recorded
provenance under the declared trusted-runtime assumptions. Physical state
reevaluation does not prove a historical execution occurred, a unique exact
solution exists, or the material model describes an experiment.

R1-2 exit additionally needs independent spatial/time/nonlinear convergence,
small-amplitude checks, target-bias DC and tail agreement, direct AC and
transient reconstruction over a quantified valid frequency range. None is
implied by this per-case validation protocol.
