# R1 evidence boundaries V3

This contract defines evidence checking for the frozen discrete R1 model. It
does not approve a new physical threshold, certify material parameters, or
identify an experimental mechanism.

## Independent choices

The caller selects the source commit, the previously fixed research request
and case-set digest, and (when applicable) an independently approved standard
digest. These are different identities. A candidate's declaration pins prove
internal consistency; editing a declaration and its pin creates a new standard
identity and does not inherit approval. A caller changing its trusted source
and anchors has selected a different candidate, not defeated an old anchor.

Declared R1 readers include the R1 scripts/modules, the R0 script used in the
R1 regression chain, and the shared transient increment implementation. Their
literal document references, including docstrings, and recursively placed R1
declaration filenames must be classified in R1_DECLARATIONS. Frozen-context
document reads also require classification. Unrelated imported-library docs
are outside this bounded rule. It is not a sandbox for arbitrary malicious
code, dynamically generated paths, or a reviewer replacing their own verifier.

## Recorded content

Step records and accepted-row containers have closed field sets. Their
physical subrecords are compared against whole reconstructed dictionaries.
Preparation, DC certificate and zero-excitation containers are classified as
well. In particular zero excitation never implies a relative dynamic-current,
full-window, three-axis or mechanism certificate. Extra scientific assertions
must not silently accompany a content-matches-recomputed result.

An accepted result cannot claim a persistence failure. Failed persistence
metadata must identify the final observed raw row and agree between its saved
representations. This checks the recorded claim; it does not replay historical
I/O. Optimizer history, timestamps and environment values are recorded
provenance. The historical maximum Jacobian nonzero count has a disclosed
sparse range and accepted-state lower bound, not a reconstructed exact value.

Byte checks of additional attachments are not scientific validation of their
contents. Verification reports list any unclassified attachments separately.

## Failed records

Every available failed row and numerical sidecar remains subject to its
applicable checks. The report distinguishes persisted rows, observed raw rows,
the expected requested schedule, and any saved physical-failure witness. A
short prefix with no observed violation does not demonstrate why the original
experiment stopped, nor prove its original execution length. Unavailable
history is explicitly unavailable, never invented. A failed record remains
ineligible for scientific acceptance even if every retained row is consistent.

## Historical verification

Legacy inspection and current physical acceptance are separate operations.
Historical labels and archived producer literals cannot select a weaker
trusted check. If a source identity cannot be independently resolved, only
byte-level historical inspection is available and source/result physics
remains unverified. A verified old source may be checked under its declared
historical contract without acquiring V3 standard approval.

The bounded compatibility set initially includes producer commits 3928259
and 815c2fe, with their own Response declaration identities and original metric
definitions. Neither is relabelled as a V3 producer. A history dispatch record
must name both producer and selected verifier identity; using an old verifier
is disclosed as old-contract checking, not current R1-2 certification.

## Completion

Implementation checks, saved-content consistency, independent standard
approval and scientific qualification are separate reported outcomes. Partial
or historical verification cannot supply a missing scientific requirement.
